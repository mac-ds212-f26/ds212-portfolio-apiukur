question What can the distribution of manuscripts found at different sites tell us about the types of texts associated with the beliefs of the commnunites around there?

Code
dead_scrolls |>
  filter(content_category != "Unidentified") |>
  ggplot(aes(x = site_parent, fill = content_category)) +
  geom_bar() +
  geom_text(stat = "count",
    aes(label = after_stat(count)),
    position = position_stack(vjust = 0.5))+
  labs(
    title = "Types of Texts Found Across Dead Sea Scrolls Sites",
    subtitle = "The Scarcity of Biblical Texts Suggests Broader Religious Traditions",
    x = "Discovery Site",
    y = "Number of Manuscripts",
    fill = "Content Category",
    caption = "Source: Week 2 TidyTuesday, Created on:09/20/26 by :Apiu Kur",
    alt = "A bar chart showing the number of manuscripts in different content categories at each Dead Sea Scrolls discovery site. The x-axis shows discovery sites and the y-axis shows the number of manuscripts. Different colors represent content categories."
  )

#observations Qumran has a large number of manuscripts and contains a wide variety of text types. Many of the manuscripts found at Qumran are non-biblical, but the site also contains manuscripts of Old Testament texts. Masada also contains mostly non-biblical manuscripts. Across the sites, non-biblical texts make up a large part of the manuscripts, although the types of texts vary between locations.

The manuscripts found at different Dead Sea Scrolls sites provide clues about the kinds of texts that were important to the communities associated with those locations. Qumran stands out because it contains a large and diverse collection of manuscripts, including both biblical and non-biblical texts. The presence of Old Testament texts alongside other types of writings suggests that the community engaged with biblical traditions while also reading and producing other kinds of texts.

The large number of non-biblical manuscripts could mean that the communities had a broader range of beliefs, practices, laws, wisdom, and other ideas that were important to the communities.
